Home/RootEd School House

Nonprofit Daycare and Learning House in Alexandria, VA

A home for learners from birth to age 12, where every child is known, loved and seen, and builds life skills through structured education and hands on, curiosity led learning.

  • Ages: birth to 12
  • Opening: January 4, 2027
Grab a spot

Who it serves

Every age gets its own space and its own rhythm.

The house is set up in learning zones, from an early learners room to a library, a maker studio and a community commons where we gather for meals and circles. Days follow a flexible rhythm instead of a bell, so care, meals, rest and each child's needs always come first.

How learning works

Child led learning in a Montessori and Reggio Emilia inspired house.

Structured, hands on learning and life skills happen in a warm, home like setting, with community building at the center. There are calm corners, and room for curious minds to explore in every room.

The School House teaches the same community centered, culturally competent curriculum as the Microschool, with the CARE framework built in, so children keep the same language, rhythms and expectations as they grow.

Growing up with RootEd

Children start here and move up to the Microschool.

School House students who are excelling and showing high levels of independence can earn a part time spot at the Project Center, 1 to 2 days a week. Older School House children can also sign up for gym and PE at the Project Center.
