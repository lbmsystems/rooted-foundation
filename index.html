What is RootEd Foundation?

RootEd Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit running two programs: the RootEd Schoolhouse for children age 2 to 12, and Root to Rise Microschool & Project Center for students age 13 and up.

In both, children learn at their own pace in small multi-age groups, with core academics taught in depth alongside practical skills: growing food, caring for body and mind, and running real projects.

How we teach

Children learn by stage, not grade. Each one can stay rooted longer where they need support, or rise ahead where they shine, building real projects and life skills from first steps to graduation.

Learning in & out of the classroom

Sustainability, wellness and entrepreneurship are part of the week, not electives. Students grow, cook, build and sell, and older students mentor younger ones.
