What is RootEd Foundation?

RootEd Foundation is a nonprofit running two schools: the RootEd School House for children from birth to 12, and Root to Rise Microschool & Project Center for students 12 and up. In both, children learn at their own pace in small multi-age groups, with core academics taught in depth alongside practical skills: growing food, caring for body and mind, and running real projects.

How we teach

Tuesdays and Thursdays focus on academics. Wednesdays and Fridays take learning into the community through field trips and projects.

Each child sets the pace

Multi-age groups let students move ahead where they're ready and slow down where they need to. Core subjects are taught in depth, not rushed.

Skills for real life

Sustainability, wellness and entrepreneurship are part of the week, not electives. Students grow, cook, build and sell.

Rooted in culture and community

We honor each child's cultural identity and teach them to look out for each other. Older students mentor younger ones.

Ready for what's next

Our academic program prepares students for the SAT, college and whatever path they choose.
