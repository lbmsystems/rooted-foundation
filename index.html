Nonprofit Daycare & Microschool in Alexandria, VA

What is RootEd Foundation?

RootEd Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit running two programs: the RootEd School House for children from birth to age 12, and Root to Rise Microschool & Project Center for students age 13 and up.

In both, children learn at their own pace in small multi age groups, with core academics taught in depth alongside practical skills: growing food, caring for body and mind, and running real projects.
