How we teach

Learning by Stage, Not Grade, from Birth to Graduation

A child can start at the School House as a baby and finish at the Microschool ready for college, work or their own enterprise, with the same community-centered teaching the whole way through.

Ages are a guide, not a gate. Within each location, a child can stay rooted in a stage longer for extra support and understanding, or rise ahead where they are excelling or show strengths, including graduating early.

Two exceptions to the age line. School House students who are excelling and showing high levels of independence earn a part-time spot at the Project Center, 1 to 2 days a week. Teens in Community Enterprise & Work Study attend the School House for both education and work.

What both sites share

The same language, rhythms and expectations at every age.

Earth-centered calendar

Planting, Growing and Harvest terms from the spring equinox to the winter solstice, with a winter distance-learning season available to all.

Every-Learner Engine

Universal design for learning, so neurodivergent and multilingual learners are planned for from the start. School counseling and therapy support the emotional side of learning at both sites.

Accreditation

Both sites are accredited by CAREvolution.
