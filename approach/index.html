How we teach

Learning by Stage, Not Grade, from Birth to Graduation

Your child can start with us as a baby and leave ready for college, a job or their own business. The way we teach stays the same the whole way through, so they always know what to expect.

C.A.R.E. Community, Ancestry, Resistance, and Expression are embedded in relationships, materials, projects, stories, family partnerships, and opportunities for learners to understand themselves and their communities.
