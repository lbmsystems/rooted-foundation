Root to Rise Microschool & Project Center · 13 and up

Project Based Microschool for Teens in Alexandria, VA

Students 13 and up strengthen their independent learning through hands on, project based work, steering their curiosity into productive learning as they rise into their future.

  • Ages: 13 and up
  • Where: 4700 King St, 5th floor, Alexandria, VA
  • Gym: shared with emPOWER Kids
Reach out to us

Who it serves

Learners 13 and up.

Age 13

Flourishing Minds

Students move up from the School House at 13.

Ages 14 to 18

Resilient Roots

Real projects, mentorship and career exploration, building toward college, work or their own business.

How learning works

Students build real projects that end in a public showcase.

Every project moves through five phases.

  1. Launch
  2. Build
  3. Deepen
  4. Create
  5. Exhibit

The space

The 5th floor of 4700 King St, split between the microschool and a gym.

Movement

The floor is shared with our emPOWER Kids program, which runs the school's own gym and PE classes. Older School House children can sign up to come here for gym and PE too.

Community free store

The Root to Rise community free store is here, run with student volunteers.

Where it leads

Ready for college, work or their own enterprise.
