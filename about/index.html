About RootEd Foundation

A Community-Rooted Nonprofit in Alexandria, VA

RootEd Foundation raises funds and grows culturally rooted, community-centered programs so every child, parent and family has the support and community they deserve to honor where they come from and rise into who they are becoming, regardless of their family's income.

Who we serve

RootEd is open to every family.

We prioritize underserved communities, marginalized Black and Brown families, and LGBTQ+ and Queer families.

Our vision: a community where everyone, including Black, Brown, bilingual, LGBTQ+ and working families, can find schools and community programs that see them, honor their history, and make room for every kind of mind and future.

How it all fits together

One nonprofit, two learning homes.

RootEd Foundation is the nonprofit that owns and runs both learning locations. It is a Virginia nonstock corporation with its own IRS 501(c)(3) status, so gifts to RootEd are tax deductible. RootEd School House is the child day center and learning house for young children and work-study students, and Root to Rise is the microschool for older children and teens.

Holds the mission and the money

Raises grants and donations, sets tuition policy, and funds scholarships so no child is turned away for cost.

Stewards the spaces

Keeps every program legally separate and in good standing, and makes sure each space is maintained and supported.

Supports the team

Educators', teaching assistants' and contractors' salaries and supplies are funded through RootEd.

Keeps the standard

Makes sure both sites teach the same community-centered, culturally competent curriculum, with the CARE framework built in to keep their CAREvolution accreditation.

Leadership

Tay Iglesias, President and Executive Director

RootEd is led by Tay Iglesias, a bilingual alternative educator with more than 15 years in child care and birth work.
