Tuition & aid

Tuition and Financial Aid

Every child gets a seat. While waitlists may happen when we're at capacity, families who need help can apply for child care subsidies or scholarship opportunities, so cost is never the reason a child misses out.

Enroll or Visit

How tuition is covered

Three ways families pay.

Qualifies for county or state child care subsidy

Subsidy

Subsidy covers tuition, and families pay little or nothing. Subsidy approval for RootEd School House (birth to 12) is pending.

Doesn't qualify for subsidy and needs help

Scholarship

Scholarship opportunities funded by RootEd donors and grants, with additional scholarships available through the CARE Education Foundation.

Self-pay

$400 a week minimum

Families paying on their own start at $400 a week.

Who gets priority: RootEd is open to every family. Scholarship and subsidy help is prioritized for underserved communities, marginalized Black and Brown families, and LGBTQ+ and Queer families.

Where the rest comes from

Fundraising keeps RootEd open to every family.

The rest of the cost comes from RootEd's fundraising: tax-deductible gifts to the 501(c)(3), grants, and ongoing support from the CARE Education Foundation. RootEd Foundation sets tuition policy and funds scholarships so no child is turned away for cost.
